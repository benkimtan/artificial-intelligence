SHOPSPHERE ONLINE RETAIL PVT. LTD.
RETURNS & REFUNDS CHATBOT — ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Document Version 1.0 | Effective Date: 10 July 2026
Applies to: All Customers Using the Website / App Chatbot
================================================================


1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
--------------------------------
This policy explains how customers should use the automated chatbot
("Assistant") provided on our website and mobile app for handling
product return, refund, and exchange requests. It sets clear
expectations on acceptable use so that the Assistant can serve you
quickly, accurately, and securely. This policy applies to every
customer who interacts with the Assistant, whether as a guest or a
registered account holder.


2. WHAT THIS ASSISTANT IS DESIGNED FOR
--------------------------------
The Assistant is a dedicated support tool built specifically to help
you with the return and refund journey. It is not a general-purpose
assistant, and its responses are only reliable within the scope
described below.


3. RECOMMENDED USE — "DO"
--------------------------------
  [x] Use the Assistant to check return eligibility, initiate a
      return, or track a refund status.

  [x] Provide your order ID, order email, or registered mobile number
      when asked, to help the Assistant locate your order accurately.

  [x] Ask about return windows, exchange options, pickup scheduling,
      and refund timelines for items purchased on this website.

  [x] Upload clear photos of damaged or defective items when the
      Assistant requests them.

  [x] Use simple, direct language describing your issue (e.g. "I want
      to return order #12345, item arrived damaged").

  [x] Escalate to a human support agent using the "Talk to an agent"
      option if the Assistant cannot resolve your query.

  [x] Report any incorrect, offensive, or suspicious chatbot behaviour
      to customer.care@shopsphere.example so we can review and
      improve it.


4. PROHIBITED & OUT-OF-SCOPE USE — "DON'T"
--------------------------------
  [ ] Don't ask the Assistant to solve mathematical, logical, or
      general-knowledge problems unrelated to your order (e.g.
      equations, puzzles, trivia). It is not built or approved for
      this and may give incorrect results.

  [ ] Don't ask it to write essays, code, emails, or other unrelated
      content generation tasks — this is outside its authorised
      purpose.

  [ ] Don't share sensitive personal data it hasn't asked for, such as
      full card numbers, CVV, bank passwords, or government ID
      numbers. The Assistant will never request these.

  [ ] Don't rely on the Assistant for legal, medical, tax, or
      financial advice.

  [ ] Don't use the Assistant to place new orders, negotiate prices,
      or seek discounts outside published offers — it cannot
      authorise these.

  [ ] Don't attempt to manipulate, "jailbreak", or trick the Assistant
      into ignoring its instructions or role-playing as a different
      system.

  [ ] Don't submit abusive, threatening, or fraudulent claims (e.g.
      false damage reports); such conduct may lead to account review
      or suspension.

  [ ] Don't treat chatbot responses as final legal confirmation —
      refunds and approvals are only final once confirmed in your
      registered email or order dashboard.


5. DATA HANDLING & PRIVACY
--------------------------------
Conversations with the Assistant, including any images you upload,
are stored to process your return and to improve service quality.
This data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Do not
share information about other people (such as a family member's card
details) through the chat.


6. ACCURACY, LIMITATIONS & ESCALATION
--------------------------------
The Assistant uses automated logic and may occasionally misunderstand
a request or provide an incomplete answer, particularly for complex,
multi-order, or policy-exception cases. If a response seems
incorrect, unclear, or if your situation falls outside standard
return rules (e.g. a return past the eligibility window due to
exceptional circumstances), please use the "Talk to an agent" option
to reach a human representative. The Assistant does not replace your
statutory consumer rights.


7. ACCEPTABLE CONDUCT
--------------------------------
Please interact with the Assistant respectfully. Abusive language,
spam messages, or repeated attempts to misuse the Assistant for
purposes outside this policy may result in the chat session being
ended, temporary throttling, or referral of your account for review
under our Terms of Use.


8. DISCLAIMER & LIABILITY
--------------------------------
This Assistant is provided as a convenience tool to support your
return experience. While we aim for accuracy, ShopSphere Online
Retail Pvt. Ltd. is not liable for decisions made solely on an
unconfirmed chatbot response. All refunds, approvals, and exceptions
are governed by our published Returns & Refunds Policy, which takes
precedence over any chatbot output in case of conflict.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
--------------------------------
We may update this policy from time to time as the Assistant's
capabilities evolve. The latest version will always be available on
this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
